# Local SEO fundamentals for small-business sites

## Title and headline discipline
Each page's H1 should contain the service and the locality once, naturally:
"Custom wedding cakes in Jaipur". Never stuff keywords — one clear phrasing
outranks five clumsy repetitions. Page titles under 60 characters.

## Location signals
Mention the neighbourhood and city in the homepage copy, the contact page,
and the footer. Spell out the full address; embed opening hours as plain
text (crawlers cannot read images). If the business serves an area, name
the area explicitly: "serving Gurgaon and South Delhi".

## Page structure crawlers reward
One H1 per page, then H2 sections in reading order. Every image needs alt
text describing the content, not the keyword. Internal links between pages
("see our menu") help both users and crawlers — every page should link to
at least two other pages in body copy.

## Words customers actually search
Use the customer's vocabulary, not the trade's: people search "birthday
cake shop near me", not "bespoke confectionery atelier". The menu/services
page should name each item the way a customer would ask for it out loud.
