# Industry-specific copy notes

## Food businesses (bakery, cafe, restaurant)
Sell the sensory moment, not the inventory: crumb, steam, crackle, the
first sip. Name signature items — a named bestseller ("The Rani Rose
Cake") becomes something customers ask for. Mention sourcing only if
specific ("butter from a Jaipur dairy", never "quality ingredients").

## Wellness (yoga, gym, salon, spa)
Lead with how the customer will feel walking out, not the equipment or
class list. Beginners are the growth market: one explicit "never done this
before?" line lowers the barrier more than any discount. Trainers and
stylists by first name with one human detail each.

## Services (repair, tutoring, design, photography)
Trust is the product. Structure copy around the worry: what breaks, what
it costs to ignore, how fast this business fixes it. State the process in
three steps — people buy certainty. Guarantees and response times beat
portfolios for first-time customers.

## Retail and boutiques
Curation is the story: why these products and not others. The owner's
taste is the brand — write the product descriptions in the owner's voice,
with one specific reason each item earned its place.
