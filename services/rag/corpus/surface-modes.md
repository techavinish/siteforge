# Surface modes — what visitor success looks like

## The four modes
Every page belongs to a mode, chosen by what the visitor is there to do.
Persuade: the visitor decides and acts — landing pages, pricing; design IS
the product; earn attention and action. Operate: the visitor completes a
task — dashboards, tools; scanability and consistency outrank expression.
Read: the visitor understands — docs, guides; structure for comprehension.
Experience: the visitor is inside the work — portfolios, galleries; the
artifact leads, the interface recedes.

## Small-business sites are Persuade surfaces
A generated business homepage is a Persuade surface: its job is a decision
(call, visit, order, book). Every section should either build the case or
lower the barrier. The services/menu page persuades through specifics and
prices; the contact page converts — friction there costs the whole site.
A photographer's portfolio page is Experience: images lead, words recede.

## The brief wins
Honor the owner's pinned tone, colors and vibe even when craft rules
suggest otherwise. Redirecting a clear brief toward generic taste is
failure. When the owner says bold, be genuinely bold — restraint applies
to decoration, never to the brand's chosen voice.

## Bounded polish
Verify in bounded passes, not loops: build fully, inspect once, fix in one
batch, confirm once, stop. Open-ended self-polish erodes more than it
improves.
